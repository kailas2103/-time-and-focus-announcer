Time & Focus Announcer

An all-in-one accessible audio timepiece and productivity suite for NVDA.

Author: Kailas Eknath Bhoye Version: 1.0 License: MIT

Overview

Time & Focus Announcer brings together an accessible spoken clock, a precision countdown timer, a full Pomodoro focus engine, and a library of premium audio earcons - all designed from the ground up for NVDA users. Every setting is reachable with a clear, direct accessible label, and every feature works the same way whether you're checking the time, running a focus session, or staying on top of your posture and hydration through the day.

Highlights

Spoken Clock Announces the time on demand, with seconds-level precision and full date on repeated presses.
Precision Countdown Timer Set a timer in hours, minutes, and seconds - supports durations of up to 24 hours (23:59:59).
Pomodoro Focus Engine A complete focus/break session cycle with configurable durations (up to 999 hours each for focus and break) and spoken transitions.
Ambient Clock Pulse An optional, unobtrusive tick - continuous or at a 3-second interval - for a steady sense of time passing.
Scheduled Announcements Automatic periodic time announcements, limited to the hours you actually want them.
12 Premium Audio Earcons A curated set of chime soundpacks to choose from, cycled with a single shortcut.
Session & Uptime Tracking Reports both the current NVDA session runtime and overall Windows uptime.
Health Reminders Gentle, optional hourly nudges to stay hydrated and mind your posture.

Keyboard Shortcuts

ShortcutAction
NVDA+Shift+T Announce the current time. Press twice quickly for exact seconds, three times for the full date.
NVDA+Control+Shift+T Set up a precision countdown timer, or check the time remaining on an active one.
NVDA+Control+Shift+P Start or stop a Pomodoro focus session.
NVDA+Control+Shift+U Report the current NVDA session runtime and Windows uptime.
NVDA+Control+Shift+M Mute or unmute automated periodic announcements.
NVDA+Control+Shift+K Turn the ambient mechanical clock pulse on or off.
NVDA+Control+Shift+B Quick-cycle through the available audio earcon soundpacks.
NVDA+Control+Shift+H Turn hourly health companion reminders on or off.
NVDA+Control+Shift+A Open the Time & Focus Announcer settings dialog.

Settings

The settings dialog (NVDA+Control+Shift+A) is organised into three tabs - General, Schedule, and Advanced - each preceded by a clear description of what that section controls, so every option is easy to find and understand with a screen reader.
